How it works

PneumoScan runs a pneumonia screening model on one chest X-ray at a time, explains which areas influenced the result, and flags when it is unsure. The model was trained across three hospitals without their images ever being shared.

About PneumoScan

PneumoScan is the demo interface for a research project on privacy-preserving medical diagnosis. It detects pneumonia patterns in chest X-rays using a DenseNet121 model trained with federated learning across three simulated hospitals, with differential privacy, secure aggregation, and encrypted, authenticated connections.

Training data: Kermany chest X-ray dataset (Hospital A) and RSNA Pneumonia Detection Challenge (Hospitals B and C). No patient data is stored in the project repository.
